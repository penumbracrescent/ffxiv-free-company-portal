# Personal Craft Planner

Deployment scope: Public Release source and fae-dev.portal-s.com only. No live deployment.

Crafting & Gathering contains Craft Planner, Timed Gathering (reserved placeholder), and Workshop & Requests. Personal lists never alter workshop projects.

Search craftable catalog items with partial names or approximate spelling. Click multiple results without closing search. Choose target quantities and the number that must be HQ. Gear Planner passes selected craftable pieces and their HQ goals; the importer asks whether to merge into the active named list or create a separate list. Owned gear is excluded.

Per-item Craft expands the selected recipe, Buy / acquire stops expansion, and Gather is offered only when a gathering source exists. Materials consolidate across targets. Multi-yield recipes round up craft executions and display extra output. Dependencies precede parents, then group by crafter; gathering routes group by catalog zone. Cycles or incomplete graphs are rejected rather than presented as complete.

Owned total includes HQ, not total plus HQ. NQ stock never satisfies an HQ output goal. HQ ingredients are not automatically required for HQ finished items: quality depends on stats, starting quality and rotation. Ordinary gil merchants supply NQ, and gathering is not a source of HQ items. The planner warns when acquisition cannot satisfy the requested quality.

Completion checks are progress markers and do not change inventory. Saved stock, recipe alternatives, locations, acquisition choices and progress belong to each named list. Up to 30 lists persist in local browser storage per account. Existing v1 lists migrate automatically. Undo/redo stores the last 20 edits in memory and resets on reload. Export/restore JSON backups are available; lists are not cloud-synced or connected to game inventory.

Crafting readiness compares the selected recipe's level and base craftsmanship/control thresholds against saved crafter profiles. Food and medicine may help meet stat thresholds. On-request official XIVAPI lookups verify master-book and specialist requirements; the player's actual unlocks cannot be read and must be confirmed in game. The Crafting Macro link passes the exact recipe and HQ/collectability or completion goal and loads the matching saved profile, without starting the solver automatically. Readiness checks are not an HQ guarantee.

Acquisition records show their imported catalog timestamps. On-request map details use official XIVAPI gathering-point or PlaceName map records, with a coordinate marker only where real coordinates are recorded. Multiple matching maps can be selected. Missing maps, coordinates, unlocks or timed-node schedules are labelled unverified; no node-timer feature is implied. Verified responses cache for one hour. Market-board prices are not queried by this personal material planner; Gear Planner retains its own Universalis comparison.

Protected APIs require a Discord-linked current verified FC character and enforce per-user request limits. Recipe graphs are bounded and read-only; no schema migration, game automation or workshop writes are introduced.

Regression coverage: named-list migration and backup validation, safe target merging, HQ stock allocation, shared demand, multi-yield recipes, buy pruning, map-coordinate bounds, exact recipe handoff and submenu highlighting. Integration checks use real catalog recipes, vendor/gathering maps and live image endpoints. Desktop/mobile UI checks cover named lists, undo/redo, HQ goals, readiness, acquisition choices, progress, persistence and gear-import confirmation.
Gathering/monster acquisition import now uses a single verified Teamcraft commit per refresh, resolving the latest staging commit before downloading its datasets. The importer replaces only its managed gathering and monster-drop records and leaves vendors, recipes, projects, workshop materials, contributions and claims alone. A failed or incomplete download retains the existing records. Normal refresh interval follows CRAFTING_CATALOG_SYNC_INTERVAL_HOURS (168 hours by default). Dev imports run in isolation without starting the FC worker or posting to Discord.

Gathering locations include job, node level, zone, sub-area, recorded gathering-area center coordinates, hidden-item status and known Eorzea-time windows. Area centers are not promises of a particular active node. Monster drops include known spawn locations, level, and FATE-only status when supported by the data; drops are not guaranteed. Unknown spawn locations and unlock requirements remain explicitly unverified. Craft Planner offers Hunt for verified monster-drop sources, separately from Gather and Buy.
Compact cards are the default; turn off Compact cards to expand per-item options. Item icons appear in searches, targets, materials, ingredients and stock controls. Route headings show shared coordinates, or identify multiple spots when coordinates differ; each acquisition item exposes its selected coordinates and gathering job without opening options. Approximate gathering-area centers remain labelled as such.

Gathering sections group by Botanist, Miner or Fisher rather than material category. Shards, crystals and clusters share a single card; ordinary nodes in existing route zones are preferred, then lower-level nodes. This convenience heuristic does not know player location or calculate shortest travel distance. User-selected locations override suggestions. The confirmed “I already have these” action records required currency quantities as stock and can be undone; stock is never assumed automatically.
Recipe tree switches the acquisition/crafting checklist to a left-to-right dependency graph across all selected targets. Shared ingredients appear once, with links to each consuming craft. The tree respects selected recipes and Craft versus Buy/Gather/Hunt expansion choices. Its structure is calculated without stock deduction so owned intermediates do not erase upstream cards; current demand and remaining quantities still use the same stock-aware calculator as the checklist. Covered or no-longer-needed cards stay visible in gray, with editable total/HQ owned fields. Full recipe quantities are labelled separately from current demand. Larger trees scroll inside their own keyboard-focusable region; no workshop or database writes are introduced.
Each recipe-tree craft card shows its elemental shards/crystals/clusters, amounts per execution and total consumption for remaining craft executions (including multi-yield rounding). Totals are direct recipe requirements, not a duplicate upstream expansion; shared raw currency cards retain the consolidated stock-adjusted acquisition total. Cards show recorded gathering coordinates and vendor name/location/coordinates/NQ gil price; extra vendors can be expanded. Missing acquisition data is labelled unverified. Checklist-selected gathering and vendor routes are respected.
Checklist rows keep the owned-total input visible alongside the total demand (“Already have / needed”); remaining demand is shown separately to avoid changing the denominator while entering stock. Owned total includes HQ; the HQ subset remains in Options. Checklist and recipe tree expose recorded acquisition badges, including a gold vendor-available indicator, gathering, monster drops and craftable alternatives. These badges indicate availability in the imported catalog, not current shop stock, market-board eligibility or player unlocks.
Tree usability controls include branch focus (ancestors and downstream consumers, excluding unrelated siblings), zoom 20–150%, fit-to-view, reset, background drag-to-pan, touch scrolling, and full-screen with Escape exit and keyboard focus containment. Raw acquisition cards use shorter heights than craft cards; extra recipe/source information is expandable. Status labels distinguish owned items, upstream items unnecessary because an intermediate is covered, missing materials, and ingredient-ready crafts. Ingredient-ready does not verify crafting stats, unlocks or HQ success.

Have enough explicitly records required stock, including the target HQ goal where present; Clear stock removes both total and HQ credit. Both actions are single Undo-able edits. Acquisition badges open source dialogs with vendor comparisons and maps, and can switch Craft/Buy/Gather/Hunt while retaining the chosen location. The pinned remaining-plan summary separates recipe currency consumption from stock-adjusted acquisition totals, reports craft executions and acquisition work, and labels vendor costs as partial estimates where prices are missing. None of these features read game inventory, purchase items, craft in game or modify workshop projects.
Recipe-tree mode expands only its containing content shell beyond the normal 1320px page cap. A ResizeObserver measures its viewport and redistributes stage columns across available width, enlarging cards up to 440px while preserving minimum 264px cards and horizontal scrolling on smaller screens. Connector endpoints follow each card’s actual width. Full-screen uses the available height; zoomed-out overviews are centered. Checklist mode restores the usual page width. Verified at 480px, 1440px and 3440px.
Manage lists consolidates new-list creation, rename/edit and delete icons, export and restore into a centered theme-variable dialog. Text entry uses Confirm/Cancel, validates nonempty names and caps names at 80 characters. The last remaining list cannot be deleted. Backup restore validates structure and the 1MB size limit before asking to replace saved lists; delete/restore/rename remain Undo-able. Gear imports use the same themed name dialog. Reset checks and bulk elemental-stock confirmation also avoid browser-native prompts. Lists remain local to this browser/account; no Discord messaging or external publication is performed.
